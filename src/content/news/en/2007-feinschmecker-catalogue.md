---
title: Vanja Dujc olive oil entered in the Feinschmecker catalogue of extra virgin olive oils
date: 2007-06-15
---

Extra virgin olive oil from the family farm Vanja Dujc entered this year too in the catalogue of the best extra virgin olive oils from all over the world Olio 2007, edited by the German gastronomic magazine Feinschmecker. This year 200 best olive oils from Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Cipher, Turkey, Australia, California, Croatia and Slovenia were elected in the catalogue. Dujc and his two olive oils, single varietal 'Maurino' and a Couvée made from about ten different varieties, are the only representatives from Slovenia. In the catalogue there is also published a broader article about Vanja Dujc family farm, about the olive grove, olive trees and about Dujc family.

Medals and awards in the 2007 season:

- **Single varietal olive oil 'Maurino':** Split (Croatia), Maslina 2007: Vice champion, Montreal, Olive d'Or 2007: Bronze Drop (third place)
- **Couvée:** Dalmatia (Croatia), Nočnjak 2007: golden medal, Izola (Slovenija), Olive Branch 2007: Silver Olive Branch
- **Lemon flavoured olive oil:** Split (Croatia), Maslina 2007: golden medal, best flavoured olive oil of the competition
