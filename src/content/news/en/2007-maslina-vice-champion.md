---
title: Vanja Dujc olive oil Vice champion of Maslina
date: 2007-02-27
---

Vanja Dujc single varietal olive oil 'Maurino' achieved the Vice champion award on the international olive oil assessment Maslina in Croatia (Split). This year 1012 olive oils, so far the greatest number, from Croatia, Slovenia, Italy, Spain, France, Bosnia, Montenegro, South Africa, Canada and Chile were competing to become the Champion of Maslina. They were assessed by three international panels. Two specialists from Slovenia, Mrs. Milena Bučar Miklavčič and Miss. Vasja Bandelj, were also making part of the international pannels. 400 olive oils achieved medals, half of them golden ones, while five oils achieved the Vice champion award. The absolute winner was Mile Vrsaljko's extra virgin olive oil from Nadin (Croatia).

Also Dujc lemon flavoured olive oil had great success on Maslina. It was named the best flavoured olive oil of the competition. Lemon flavoured olive oil is produced with simultaneous processing of olives and lemons of biological production. Dujc was given the recipe by his friend olive grower from Italy. This excellent oil, recommended especially for fish and vegetable plates, is still quite difficult to buy in Slovenia.
