---
title: "'Vanja' olive oils entered this year too in two well-known catalogues of extra virgin olive oils"
date: 2008-05-28
---

German gastronomic magazine *Feinschmecker* and *Slow Food* from Tuscany have chosen olive oil ‘Vanja’ to enter in their respective catalogues of best olive oils once again. In *Olio*, the catalogue published by the *Feinschmecker*, are present two of our oils, Couvée and single varietal Maurino. The last one managed to come even in the ‘best 35’ group. In the catalogue of the Tuscan Slow Food *Guida agli extravergini* Maurino is elected in the special ‘Group of Excellence’, together with 45 other oils. As the authors explain, in this group enter extra virgin olive oils of supreme quality that have somehow managed to leave an especially positive impression on the team of experts that assessed the oils.
