---
title: Bronze and gold for Vanja Dujc olive oil
date: 2007-04-25
---

Extra virgin olive oil 'Vanja' achieved golden medal on the international competition Nočnjak, organised annually by the Dalmatia Olive Growers Organisation (Savez maslinara Dalmacije). This year Nočnjak was taking place on the island Brač. This is already the fifth consecutive golden medal from Nočnjak for this extra virgin olive oil.

Vanja Dujc and his oil had great success also in Canada. On the debut of the international olive oil assessment Olive d'Or in Montreal, the single varietal 'Maurino' achieved third place and the Bronze drop award in the most popular category of medium fruity. We are especially proud of the success because all of 144 extra virgin olive oil were of a high quality.
