**MAURINO**

- extraordinarily aromatic olive, with an intensive fruity aroma, the sensation of pungent and bitter are pleasantly intensive
- aroma: of freshly mown grass, fresh almonds and green fruit
- flavour: of green banana, green apple and herbs
- we recommend it with: fish starters, risottos, pasta with seafood, grilled white meat, warm vegetable starters or matured cheese.

**LECCINO**

- delicate and mild oil, a bit more intensive than itrana, pleasantly spicy
- aroma: of green (freshly mown grass and fresh herbs)
- flavour: of freshly crushed olive leaves and green vegetables (tomato, herbs, grass)
- we recommend it with: fish (grilled fish, grilled shrimps), green salads.

**ISTRIAN WHITE**

- the most intensive and robust of all our oils, decisively bitter and pungent
- aroma: of freshly mown grass, olive leaves and green vegetables
- taste: of crushed olive leaves
- we recommend it with: vegetable soups and minestrones, pasta, pork meat or roast beef.

**ITRANA**

- the most sweet and delicate of all our oils
- we recommend it with: young cheese, cold starters, rice, bread or salads
- aroma: of honey and wild flowers
- taste: of ripe apple, sweet almonds, dried fruit and vanilla.
