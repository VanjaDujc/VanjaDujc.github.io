**TABLE OLIVES IN SALT WATER**

- green olives of the variety Ascolana Tenera
- the local sea salt from Piran salt marshes is used for the brine
- the fruit is medium bitter and solid
- the brine is not strong, therefore the olives are not very salty and preserve their own flavour
- due to the small percentage of the salt used in brine the product is pasteurised.
