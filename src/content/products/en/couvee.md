**COUVÉE**

- about 16 different varieties are mixed together in this oil
- the flavours, characteristic of a single variety, combine one with another, creating an oil of classic and noble taste, with well present yet balanced sensation of bitter and pungent
- aroma: of freshly mown grass, crushed olive leaves and herbs
- flavour: of green vegetables (green tomato, artichoke) and herbs
- we recommend it: with risottos, pasta, red meat or poultry, vegetable soups and salads.
