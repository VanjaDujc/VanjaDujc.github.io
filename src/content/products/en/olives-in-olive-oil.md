**TABLE OLIVES IN OLIVE OIL**

- black olives of the variety Leccino in extra virgin olive oil couvée
- olives are processed naturally in salt from the local Piran salt marshes
- the bitterness is dissolved from olives without washing them in water, therefore the olives manage to preserve intense flavour
- olives are not acid
- recommended for: cold starters; if pitted they are delicious in tomato sauce or cold rice salads.
