---
title: Istrian home made bread with seeds
order: 16
course: starter
ingredients: |-
  - 250g whole wheat flour
  - 250g white flour
  - 2dl extra virgin olive oil
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon sugar
  - 1 pocket of dried yeast (leaven)
  - 200gr of mixed seeds (sunflower, pumpkin, flax)
---

Mix all the ingredients until you get smooth homogeneous dough. Add some water if necessary. Leave the dough an hour to rise. Put the dough in a baking tin and spread it about 0,5-1cm high, then cut into pieces with a knife. Leave it some time to rise again and bake 35-40 min at 190°C.
