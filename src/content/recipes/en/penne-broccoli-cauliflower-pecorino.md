---
title: Penne with broccoli, cauliflower and pecorino cheese
order: 9
---

**Ingredients**:

- 500 gr penne rigate pasta
- ½ head of cauliflower
- 1 head broccoli
- 4 cloves garlic
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- pecorino cheese
- salt
- 1 chilli pepper

**Instructions**:  
Put a big pan half full of water on the stove and bring the water to boil. Wash and cut the cauliflower and broccoli into florettes. When the water is boiling, add salt and 1 tablespoon of olive oil, penne Reigate pasta and cauliflower. Cook for about 5 minutes, than add broccoli. Cook until the pasta is ‘al dente’ (not overcooked). Strain and drizzle with extra virgin olive oil. Chop garlic and chilli pepper, put 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a pan, add chilli pepper and and fry for 2 minutes, than add garlic and fry until sizzling. Reduce the heat, add pasta with vegetables and fry for a minute or two. Serve, drizzle with extra virgin olive oil and sparkle with pecorino cheese.
