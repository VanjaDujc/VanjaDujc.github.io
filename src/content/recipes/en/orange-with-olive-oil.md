---
title: Sliced orange with sheep cheese
order: 4
ingredients: |-
  - 2 oranges
  - 100 g sheep cheese
  - salt
  - 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil - monocultivar "Maurino"
  - 1 tablespoon of fresh basil, finely shredded
---

Cut the oranges into slices, app. 0,5 cm thick. Dice the sheep cheese. Arrange on a serving platter. Salt the oranges, sprinkle the basil over the cheese and drizzle oranges and cheese with oil just before serving.
