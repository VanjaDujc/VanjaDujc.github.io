---
title: Rice salad
order: 3
course: starter
oil: orange-oil
ingredients: |-
  - 2 cups of rice
  - 100 gr mocarella cheese
  - black table olives
  - 1 fresh red pepper
  - 1 fresh yellow pepper
  - 1 apple
  - 1 mandarin
  - 1 little tin of corn
  - 4 pickled cucumbers
  - extra virgin olive oil
  - 2 tablespoons orange flavoured olive oil
  - fresh parsley, finely shredded
  - pepper
  - salt
---

Cook rice until it is 'al dente' (not overcooked), strain it and drizzle whit extra virgin olive oil. Let it cool. Peel apple and mandarin and dice them to small pieces. Dice peppers, cucumbers and mocarella. Remove the bones from olives. Dice the olives. Mix all the ingredients to the rice, add salt and pepper, sprinkle with parsley and drizzle with orange flavoured olive oil, stir well and put in the refrigerator for an hour before served.
