---
title: Roasted apples with honey and lemon flavoured olive oil
order: 13
---

**Ingredients**:

- 4 apples
- 4 tablespoons honey
- 4 tablespoons almonds, cut on small pieces
- icing sugar
- cinnamon
- lemon flavoured olive oil
- butter

**Instructions**:  
Wash apples and cut the pips out so that you make a hole in the middle of each apple. Mix almonds with 4 tablespoons of honey and put some mixture inside each hole. Arrange apples on the prepared tray. Heat the oven to moderate (180°C), sift the icing sugar and cinnamon over the apples. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until crisp and lightly browned. Arrange apples on small plates and drizzle with lemon flavoured olive oil.
