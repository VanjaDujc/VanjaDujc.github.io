---
title: Tagliatelle with truffles
order: 8
---

**Ingredients**:

- 500 gr taglatelle pasta
- 2 fresh or frozen truffles
- truffle flavoured olive oil
- extra virgin olive oil
- salt
- parmiggiano cheese

**Instructions**:  
Put a big pan full of water on the stove and bring the water to boil. Add some salt and 1 spoon olive oil, then pasta and cook it until it is ‘al dente’ (not overcooked). Strain, drizzle with extra virgin olive oil and serve on plates. Grate half of the truffle on each plate, drizzle with truffle flavoured olive oil and sprinkle with grated parmiggiano. The truffles are sliced with a special knife for truffles. If you don’t have one, you can use the grater. If you use frozen truffles, take them from the refrigerator the moment before serving them.
