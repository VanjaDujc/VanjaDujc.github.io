---
title: Grilled peaches with sweet sauce
order: 14
course: dessert
oil: orange-oil
ingredients: |-
  - • 4 ripe peaches
  - 2 tablespoons of honey
  - 50 gr almonds
  - ¼ cup of orange flavoured olive oil
  - 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  - 1 lemon
---

Chop almonds, mix them with honey and orange flavoured olive oil. Squeeze lemon and mix the lemon juice with almonds, honey and oil. Cut peaches on half, remove the bone. Put extra virgin olive oil in a pan, add peaches and roast on a moderate temperature until the peaches become golden brown and softer. Arrange on a plate, spread the sweet sauce over each half of the peach and serve hot.
