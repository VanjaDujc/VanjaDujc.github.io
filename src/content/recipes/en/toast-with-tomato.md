---
title: Tostada with tomato
order: 2
course: starter
ingredients: |-
  - four slices of whole wheat bread
  - 3 ripe tomatoes
  - 4 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil
  - salt
---

Sparkle slices of bread with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and roast them in a pan or oven until they achieve golden colour. Grate tomatoes, add a bit of salt and what is left of the remaining olive oil and spread it on the bread.
