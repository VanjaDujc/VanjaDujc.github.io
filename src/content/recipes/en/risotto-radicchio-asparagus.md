---
title: Risotto with radicchio rosso di Treviso and asparagus
order: 7
ingredients: |-
  - 2 cups rice
  - 200 gr fresh young asparagus stalks
  - a glass of white wine
  - ½ of onion
  - salt
  - 3 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil
  - truffle flavoured olive oil
  - parmiggiano cheese
  - 2 bunches of radicchio rosso di Treviso
---

Chop the onion. Put the oil in a pan, add chopped onion and fry it until it becomes translucent. Wash radicchio di Treviso, cut it into thin wedges, than add to the onion. Reduce the heat, salt, add a bit of wine, cover with pot lid and sauté for about 5 minutes. Add the rice, increase the heat and roast for a minute, than add a cup of water, reduce the heat and cook until the water evaporates. Add the remaining wine and cook until the rice is ‘al dente’ (not overcooked). Add some water and salt in necessary while cooking. Serve, sparkle with parmiggiano cheese and drizzle with truffle flavoured olive oil.
