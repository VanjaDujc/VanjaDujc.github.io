---
title: Bacalao "in bianco" a la Vanja Dujc
order: 1
ingredients: |-
  - 500 g dried cod or bacalao
  - 3 leaves fresh bay leaf
  - 1 teaspoon black pepper in grains
  - 15 g fresh parsley
  - 2 carrots
  - 10 dkg celery
  - ½ dl extra virgin olive oil – "Vanja" blend
  - salt
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  - 15g chopped fresh parsley
---

Remove the excess salt from the cod by placing it in a large bowl with cold water and leaving it to soak for 12 hours, changing the water 2 times. Put the cod in a pan and cover it with water. Wash parsley and carrots, peel celery. Add bay leaf, grains of pepper, parsley, celery and carrots to the water. Bring to the boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes. Drain well. When cool enough for handle, remove the skin and bones from the cod and flake it with your fingers to a bowl. Salt and pepper, sprinkle the parsley over the bacalao flakes and drizzle it with oil. Refrigerate for some hours before serving.  

We recommend to serve this dish with dried tomatoes in olive oil and table olives in salt water "Vanja".
